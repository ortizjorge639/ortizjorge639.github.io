# Knowledge that earns its place

Capture freely. Promote carefully. Keep the evidence close.

## Keeping a thought from becoming a conclusion too soon

The workflow distinguishes keeping material from endorsing it. Capture has no review gate: a passing thought can remain a dated raw note and be found later. Durable knowledge has a higher bar. This reduces the conceptual risk of presenting an isolated idea as an established conclusion without pretending that recurrence makes a claim true.

## Eligibility is not approval

The documented recurrence floor is at least two independent source units separated by three or more calendar days. Event-aware deduplication prevents multiple representations of the same event from manufacturing extra support. Passing that floor makes a candidate eligible for assessment, not automatically correct or ready to promote.

An agent prepares a focused claim with exact supporting passages and considers existing knowledge and earlier decisions. The person can accept, correct, reject or defer, with a reason retained in the decision record. The helper requires confirmation of the displayed proposal before recording the outcome. Recording a decision is separate from changing a knowledge page; it does not rewrite raw sources or autonomously update the agent's instructions. Semantic assessments still need judgment, and a confirmation flag is not an authentication mechanism.

## Retrieval that exposes its support

The core substrate is Markdown and links rather than a custom database or graph UI. Current grounded retrieval and review paths use Python; legacy Ruby helpers remain for capture and discovery. Raw-note search and accepted-knowledge retrieval are different operations.

A deterministic, read-only packet builder scans accepted knowledge, resolves its supporting evidence, and emits only complete records that fit a context budget. It validates source metadata and distinguishes no match, budget exclusion and invalid evidence. It does not generate the final answer or call a model. An answer-generating host still has to interpret the packet faithfully; citations alone do not prove that interpretation is correct.

## Hypothetical illustration — not a bundled test

Imagine a notebook containing an invented idea about a reusable workshop checklist. One note does not become a lasting page. Independent discussion several days later could make the idea eligible for assessment, while duplicate exports of the first discussion would not. A human might still reject the proposed page as unsupported or unnecessary. This invented notebook is not a supplied fixture, an executed recurrence test or a quotation from a real vault.

## Evidence and limits

The reviewed README, product definition and review schema describe source preservation, recurrence, bounded citations and explicit decisions. Historical regression and experiment claims were not independently rerun. No real vault, pitch notes, decision history or generated packet was opened for this case study.

This is an on-demand file-and-script workflow, not a continuously running knowledge service. Documented import adapters read local exports; live API connectors are outside the described implementation. Link visualization can be delegated to an existing editor. The narrative claims no improvement in cognition, recall, answer quality or downstream work. The valuable boundary is a reviewable path from raw material to maintained knowledge, with the original evidence still available privately.
